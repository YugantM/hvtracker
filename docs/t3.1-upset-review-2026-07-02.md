## Key finding: one dimension dominates

`package_provenance_drift` contributes ±4–5 points and appears as the largest
term in **every** top swing, both directions. The other three runtime
dimensions are small (mcp ≤ +2.0, ext-deps ≥ −3.0, tool-surface ≥ −2.5).
In effect, v2 as currently calibrated is mostly a provenance-drift reweight
with runtime garnish. Before this drives production rank, the drift signal's
false-positive rate needs review — a "mismatch" status is worth −5.0 only if
mismatches are reliably real, not packaging noise.
